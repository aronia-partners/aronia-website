Vincit USA · building the U.S. business from zero↗
From first customer Logitech to eight figures in U.S. revenue.

In 2016, I moved to California to build the U.S. business for Vincit, a publicly traded European software and design company. We had a strong track record in Europe, but no U.S. customers, local reputation, or established go-to-market motion.

I landed our first customer, Logitech, then built the U.S. go-to-market from the ground up. We focused the positioning, built local credibility, won category-leading customers, and used three acquisitions to accelerate growth. Revenue doubled each of the first four years, reaching eight figures within five years.

Privv · built from zero↗
From an idea to $1M ARR.

I co-founded Privv and built a SaaS project management platform for capital projects. Privv helps building owners and owner's representatives manage budgets, schedules, cash flow, and project workflows in one place, replacing spreadsheets, email, and legacy tools.

We identified the initial market, learned where the ICP wasn't working, pivoted to a stronger segment, and built a repeatable sales motion around it. That sales engine took Privv from zero to $1M ARR, with more than $4 billion in projects managed through the platform.

About Ville Houttu

I came to America to build a U.S. business too.

I'm a Finnish engineer and entrepreneur. In 2016, I moved to California with a simple mandate: build the U.S. business for a European software and design company.

We had a strong company and track record in Europe. In America, we had to earn it again. I landed our first customer, built the local team and go-to-market motion, and learned firsthand how differently relationships, credibility, sales, and growth work when you cross the Atlantic.

Over the next several years, I built a team of more than 60 people, completed three acquisitions, established a U.S. leadership team, and helped turn the operation into a successful American business.

Along the way, the companies I led made the Inc. 5000 three years in a row and were recognized by Fast Company and Inc. for the workplaces we built. I was named Irvine Entrepreneur of the Year and later became an EY Entrepreneur of the Year finalist. I've also shared what I learned through TEDx, Forbes, TechCrunch, Inc., and Adam Grant's WorkLife podcast.

That's the experience I bring to Aronia. I've sat on the European side of the table and built the American business on the other side.
